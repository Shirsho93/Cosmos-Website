Chapter 01

The Solar System

A live, interactive orrery. Drag to orbit, click the scene then scroll to zoom, and pick any world to learn about it. Sizes and distances are compressed so everything fits on screen.

Chapter 02

Planet atlas

Hover over, tap or tab to any world to see its vital statistics. The spheres show relative size. The gaps between them are not to scale.

Chapter 03

Your weight across worlds

Gravity changes from world to world. Enter your weight and see how heavy you would feel standing on each one. (On the gas giants, “standing” means hovering at the cloud tops.)

Chapter 04

Riding a beam of light

Light travels 299,792 km every second, the fastest anything can go. Even so, crossing the Solar System takes hours. Launch a photon from the Sun and watch it go.

Chapter 05

The Milky Way

A barred spiral of 100–400 billion stars, about 100,000 light-years across. Our Sun sits in a minor arm roughly 26,000 light-years from the centre and takes about 230 million years to make one lap.

Chapter 06

Zooming out

Drag the slider to step outward, from a single planet to the observable universe.

Chapter 07

A keyhole to the early universe

Hubble stared at a tiny patch of sky, smaller than a grain of sand held at arm’s length, and found it full of thousands of galaxies. Move the lens to explore.
